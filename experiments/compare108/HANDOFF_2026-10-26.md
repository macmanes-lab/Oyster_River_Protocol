## 4. Mechanism: where the genes go

Read from `scripts/twotrack_select.py` and `oyster.py` (4.0.0):

1. **Two-track selection** (`twotrack_select.py`): one representative per best-hit
   Swiss-Prot gene among pooled contigs over 200 bp, plus up to two distinct, expressed
   copies per gene. No gene is lost here.
2. **Rescue** (`make_list3`, `make_list5`, `posthack`, `oyster.py:2660-2725`): any gene
   among the components' hits but not the selection's gets one contig back, including
   contigs of 200 bp or less. Still no gene is lost.
3. **`cd-hit-est -c .98`** (`oyster.py:2727`): removes any contig >=98% identical (over the
   shorter sequence's full length) to part of a longer one, **regardless of gene**. This is
   the only step after selection that removes a contig with a hit.
4. **Gene count** (`hits_for`, `extract_gene_ids`): distinct Swiss-Prot names among all hit
   lines, which DIAMOND limits with `--top 0.1` to hits within 10% of each contig's best.

When step 3 folds a gene-X contig into a longer contig whose best hit is a related protein
scoring more than 10% higher, X falls out of that contig's hit list and out of the count,
though its sequence is still in the assembly. The unique-gene metric counts names, not
sequence, and is conservative for a merged assembly whose contigs are longer than its inputs'.

## 5. Would `cd-hit-est -c .99` help? (decided: no change)

Lost genes binned by identity to their ORP contig (blastn identity x coverage, approximating
cd-hit's global identity):

| Identity to ORP contig | Genes | Share | At `-c .99` |
|---|---|---|---|
| >=99% | 32,103 | 70% | still collapsed |
| 98-99% | 4,914 | 11% | kept |
| 95-98% | 2,593 | 6% | unaffected |
| <95% or no match | 6,089 | 13% | unaffected |

- At most about 4,900 genes back across 59 runs, median 59 per run (range 7-303): under 1%
  of a run's count.
- 15,273 (33%) are exact containments (100% id, 100% cov), which no threshold separates.
- The cost falls on every contig pair 98-99% identical, roughly the divergence of
  heterozygous alleles and young homeologs, so contig counts and BUSCO duplication would
  likely rise, most in the polyploid plants. Not measured: the run directories no longer
  hold the input to that last CD-HIT-EST step.
- A targeted alternative, not pursued: let cd-hit-est not drop a contig that is the last
  carrier of its best-hit gene when the absorbing contig does not hit that gene. That would
  recover most of the masked 78% without keeping allelic copies. Either change alters ORP's
  output and would need a major version bump.
